# Repo Instructions

- Work directly on `main`.
- Do not create branches.
- Do not open PRs.
- Commit and push directly to `main`.
- Keep the private example corpus out of git. It belongs in `prompts/private-examples.md`, `PRIVATE_EXAMPLE_CORPUS`, or other server-only storage.
